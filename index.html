About CLUB10CHICKEN

CLUB10CHICKEN has become the go-to spot for chicken lovers in Puliyankudi and surrounding areas. What started as a small shop has grown into a beloved local brand known for quality, taste, and unbeatable value.

We use only the freshest ingredients, premium chicken, and our secret blend of spices to create flavors that keep customers coming back for more. Every piece is made to order — crispy on the outside, juicy on the inside.
